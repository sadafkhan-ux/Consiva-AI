# The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: every section, when it applies, and what a website scan can see

Sources read: `knowledge_sources/DPDP Act 2023.pdf` (22 of 2023) and the commencement notification G.S.R. 843(E) of 13 Nov 2025 (`DPDP Enforcement Timeline .pdf`). Parameter references (A1, B2 and so on) are those of the Scanner Score (`docs/SCANNER_SCORE.md`).
A mapping, not a legal determination. Whether an organisation complies depends on records and practice that a website scan cannot see.

**Commencement (G.S.R. 843(E))**
- 13 Nov 2025 (on publication): s.1(2), s.2, ss.18 to 26, s.35, ss.38 to 43, s.44(1) and (3).
- 13 Nov 2026 (one year): s.6(9) and s.27(1)(d).
- 13 May 2027 (eighteen months): ss.3 to 5, s.6 (the rest), ss.7 to 17, s.27 (except (1)(d)), ss.28 to 34, ss.36 and 37, s.44(2).

## What this shows
- Of the 44 sections, the ones a public website scan can say anything about are **ss.4, 5, 6, 8(2), 8(5), 8(9), 8(10), 9, 11 to 14 and 16**. The rest concern the Board, procedure, penalties, rules and obligations that live in an organisation's records and decisions.
- Nearly every operative duty on a Data Fiduciary starts on **13 May 2027**. Until then a scan reports readiness, never a current breach (Scope RP-12, GR-05).
- Section 8(7), retention, has no parameter in the Scoring Mechanism, so what a notice says about retention is no longer scored. The earlier ten-check engine did score it.
- The Rules (DPDP Rules 2025) add the detail for notice (rule 3), consent managers (rule 4), security (rule 6), children (rule 10), rights and grievance (rules 9 and 14) and cross-border transfer (rule 15). They are in `knowledge_sources/DPDP Rules.pdf` and are not tabulated here.
